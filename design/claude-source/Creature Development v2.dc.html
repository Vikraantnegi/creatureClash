5Faceted style rules
Volumes. Every mass is a 6–9-point facet polygon. Legs are straight 4-point rods; type marks (flame, fin, leaf, spark, horn) are flat triangles. No curves anywhere.
Light. Fixed top-left. Each volume gets one shade wedge (lower-right, inset 8%) and one light wedge (upper-left, 55%). Triangles stay flat — they are the only un-lit shapes, which makes type marks pop.
Ink. 3 units of 200 on all volumes, #26201D, never coloured. Eyes 1.8. Wedges have no ink.
Face kit. Eye = white facet + diamond pupil offset toward the opponent. Brow = one slab, 8–16 long; confident pitches it +14°, defeated −18°. Mouth = one line; confident lifts 42%, defeated drops 45%. Defeated eyes become a single slanted bar.
Density. 14–22 shapes per creature. Voltik and Ashkit at the top (thin parts need more pieces), Fernlet and Brookfin at the bottom.
Minimums. Limb 8 units, foot pad 15 × 7, smallest triangle 10 tall, eye radius ≥ 3.8. All survive a 64 px roster tile and a 40 px portrait.
