6Palette & silhouette guidance
Shared ink. One outline colour #26201D on every creature; weight 3 units of 200 (Faceted) or 2.4 (Soft Ink). Never coloured outlines.
Three values per creature — base, shade, light — plus one accent for the type mark. Shade is always the base at ~60% lightness, same hue; light at ~130%. Keeps all six in one lighting world.
Type is a shape, colour is a reminder. Fire = flame triangles (tall crest on Ashkit, stubby back ridge on Emberhorn). Water = fin triangles + skirt. Rock = chamfered slabs and spine plates. Grass = leaf cap + hanging fringe. Electric = bolt ears + zigzag limbs.
Silhouette family. Ashkit diagonal wedge · Brookfin vertical column · Slate horizontal slab · Fernlet dome · Voltik thin zigzag · Emberhorn low anvil. No two share a bounding-box aspect ratio; all six pass a 32 px black-fill test.
The two Fire creatures. Ashkit is small, tall-crested, legs longer than body, weight forward on toes. Emberhorn is wide, horned, head below shoulder line, legs shorter than body depth. Their hues also sit apart (orange/yellow vs oxblood/charcoal) as a secondary cue.
Eyes. One eye style per direction; pupil offset toward the opponent. Eye size scales with Speed: Voltik largest, Emberhorn smallest.
7Proposed asset specification
Art space
200 × 200 units, ground line y = 170 (85%), feet centred x = 100, faces right
Export
PNG, transparent, square. 256 pt base → 256 / 512 / 768 px (@1x @2x @3x). SVG source kept.
Max height
150 units (75%). Brookfin and Voltik hit the cap; Slate ≈ 80, Emberhorn ≈ 105
Padding
≥ 10 units clear on all sides in every pose; defeated pose may not exceed the frame
Poses
neutral · ready · victory · defeated — 4 stills × 6 = 24 sprites
Idle
Code-driven: neutral sprite, translateY ±2%, 1.6 s ease-in-out loop. No extra frames
Portrait
128 × 128 pt, transparent, cropped from neutral at the listed crop; 6 files
Naming
creatures/ashkit/ashkit_ready@2x.png · portraits/ashkit_portrait@2x.png
Mirroring
Never export mirrored files — opponent side uses scaleX(-1) in the view
Outline
3 / 200 units → 3.84 px @1x. Scale with export, never fixed-pixel
Layers (opt.)
tail · far limbs · body · near limbs · head · type mark — if later rigged, export as 6 PNG layers, same canvas
QA
Black-fill silhouette must be identifiable at 32 px; each pose must differ from neutral in bounding box, not only in face
